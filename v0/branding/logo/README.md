# 11blog logo

The 11blog logo combines a geometric double-one mark with a compact, mono-inspired `blog` wordmark, allowing the complete lockup to read as 11blog without repeating the number. A small green square acts as a signal or cursor: a restrained reference to the product-engineering and technology focus without turning the identity into a literal technology icon.

The construction follows the wider brand direction:

- sharp geometry and square corners;
- foreground white on the default dark background;
- green used as a small, deliberate signal;
- custom geometric letterforms informed by the interface's monospaced typography;
- a simple silhouette that remains legible at small sizes.

## Assets

- [`11blog-logo.svg`](./11blog-logo.svg) — primary horizontal lockup with a transparent background, intended for `#0A0A0A` or another sufficiently dark surface.
- [`11blog-logo-dark.svg`](./11blog-logo-dark.svg) — primary lockup with padding on the default `#0A0A0A` background.
- [`11blog-mark.svg`](./11blog-mark.svg) — standalone square mark on the default dark background, suitable for avatars and compact placements.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the green signal square.
- Use the horizontal lockup at no less than 160 pixels wide.
- Use the standalone mark at no less than 24 × 24 pixels.
- Do not round the container, stretch the artwork, add shadows, or change individual colors.
- The wordmark is already converted to vector shapes and does not require a font file.
